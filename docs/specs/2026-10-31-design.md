# Perfume catalogue: Design

Status: draft for review (2026-10-31). Sister app to **Seeds** (`~/code/seeds`, live at seeds-chi.vercel.app). Wherever this document says "as in Seeds", the Seeds code and `docs/decisions.md` there are the reference.

Tags: **[user]** = user decided · **[my call]** = Claude's call, open to revisit · **[seeds]** = carried over from a Seeds decision.

## Why

Someone owns a shelf of perfume, perhaps 40 bottles plus a pile of samples and discovery sets. They want three things:

1. **Get it all in, fast.** Every bottle, sample and set, in a structured record, with as little typing as Seeds needed. [user]
2. **Decide what to wear today.** Suggestions based on the day, the time, the season and the weather. [user]
3. **Remember how it went.** A diary of what was worn each day, with notes and a star rating, linked back to each perfume. [user]

On top of that sit a searchable catalogue, stats, gaps in the collection, a wishlist, "smells like" links and layering ideas. [user]

**Success:** cataloguing the shelf feels as quick as Seeds, Today gets opened every day, and the diary becomes a record the user actually keeps.

## Goals

- **Capture.** Bottle mode with automatic capture, a samples mode (one photo of many vials) and a discovery-set mode (one photo of the box creates the set and its samples). [user]
- **AI details.** Claude identifies each perfume and fills in what's known about it. Each field is labelled with where it came from. [user]
- **Collection.** Search and filter, as in Seeds, with switches for Owned, Sets and Wishlist. [user]
- **Today.** Three picks for right now, with a reason for each, a "wear" button, and nudges. [user]
- **Diary.** Log one or more wears per day, each with a 1–5 star rating and free-text notes. Month view now; year view later. Each perfume's page pulls in its notes. [user]
- **Stats.** Totals, estimated value, a breakdown by scent family, by season and by house, worn vs. neglected, and gaps. [user]
- **Wishlist, smells like, layering.** [user]
- **Multi-tenant from day one.** Each **collection** (household) is private, and the facts about each perfume are shared. [user]
- **Launch users:** one collection with **two logins** (the person whose perfumes they are, and the developer for support). [user] They act as one user: **no per-person features** — no "who wore it", no per-person views. [user] No public sign-up; collection membership is set up manually (SQL or a script), with no invite UI. [my call] Public launch concerns (AI spend limits per user, abuse protection on the shared table, currencies other than £, southern-hemisphere seasons, a weather service with higher limits) are deferred.

## Non-goals

- Running-low alerts. [user: "too much maintenance"]
- Tracking how full a bottle is. [my call: same reason]
- Sharing or social features.
- Price tracking or marketplace lookups.
- Barcode or online database lookups.
- Offline mode. [seeds]
- A native app (this is a PWA). [seeds]

## Anti-goals

- **Scanning as the centrepiece.** Scanning should get the job done and get out of the way: a header icon, not a big button. [user]
- **Upkeep chores** the user has to remember.
- **Confident wrong identifications** saved without review.
- **Today feeling generic** or repeating the same three picks every week.

## Constraints and stack

All of these follow Seeds unless noted. [user: "infra should copy seeds"]

- **Monorepo:** pnpm + Turborepo, with `apps/web` (Next.js 16 App Router, React 19, Tailwind 4, `cacheComponents` on), `packages/core` (domain logic and tests) and `supabase/` (migrations).
- **Hosting:** Vercel (Hobby). The Vercel project root is `apps/web`. Supabase handles Postgres, Auth, Storage and RLS.
- **New projects:** new Supabase and Vercel projects. A dedicated Anthropic key, as in Seeds.
- **Auth:** email + password and Google, as in Seeds. No public sign-up; accounts and membership are created manually for now. Use the same password-recovery token-hash flow and `/auth/confirm` hardening.
- **PWA:** mobile first, online only, and a service worker that only provides the offline fallback page. [seeds]
- **AI:** Claude vision with structured outputs, server-side, triggered with `after()`. Start with `claude-sonnet-5-5`, as in Seeds' extraction. Use refusal fallbacks, a capped number of retries and server-side error logging. [seeds]
- **Weather:** Open-Meteo, attributed as in Seeds. Only the user's approximate location is stored.
- **Git:** the git author email must be the Vercel-linked one, or deploys are blocked. [seeds]

## Design direction [user]

- **Direction A, "Apothecary label":**
  - **Palette:** cream paper `#F5EEE0` (page) and `#FFFAF0` (cards), oxblood `#6E2A2A` (primary), gold `#E8C46A` (accent), dark ink `#3A1D1D`, muted `#8A6A55`, rule lines `#D8C8A8`, dusty rose `#C98A8A` (secondary).
  - **Typography:** Bodoni Moda for display (italic for headings), Figtree for UI text.
  - **Cards:** engraved-label style with a fine inner rule.
- **Signature motif:** the **scent pyramid** (top / heart / base), filled in to show where a perfume's weight sits. It appears on Today picks, perfume pages and the collection list.
- **App icon:** monogram option **C**. The first letter of the app's name in **upright** Bodoni with a gold foil gradient, inside a double gold ring (fine outer ring, hairline inner ring), on an oxblood radial vignette. The letter is centred optically. The reference mockup is `.superpowers/brainstorm/*/content/monogram-v2.html`.
- **Name:** **still open.** "Flacon" is the working placeholder. The icon letter follows the final name. Infrastructure (repo, Supabase project, Vercel project) is named `perfume-catalog` so the display name can change freely later. [my call, after builder review]
- **Navigation:**
  - Four tabs: **Today · Collection · Diary · Stats**.
  - The header has the wordmark, a small **scan icon** and a settings icon.
  - No central scan button. [user]
  - Review is reached from a notice on Today and from the scan screen, as in Seeds.
- **Mockups:** `apothecary-v2.html` (Today, Diary, perfume page) and `apothecary-v3-extras.html` (Wishlist, Stats, smells like, layering) in the same folder.

## Data model (shape, not final schema)

Shared and private records are split the way Seeds splits species/variety from packets. [seeds pattern, my call]

- **`perfumes`**, shared:
  - **Identity:** house, name, concentration (EDT/EDP/parfum/extrait…), flanker or edition, perfumer(s), release year, house type (designer/niche/indie).
  - **Scent:** scent family and sub-family, main accords, notes split into top/heart/base.
  - **Performance and wear:** longevity, sillage/projection, seasons, time of day, occasions, how it's marketed by gender.
  - **Status:** discontinued or reformulated flags, and a typical retail price per ml (for value estimates).
  - **Rules:** users never edit these records directly. A user's edits are stored on their own item. Matching uses normalised house + name + concentration, as in Seeds' `normalise_name`.
- **`collections`** and **`collection_members`**: a collection is the tenant; members are users (owner or member). Every private table below belongs to a collection, not a user. Each user is in one collection for now. [my call, from "two emails on the same account"]
- **`items`**, one per physical thing in a collection:
  - **Links:** collection, perfume, and the set it came from (if any).
  - **Format:** full bottle, travel, decant or sample.
  - **Purchase:** size (ml), price, date and shop.
  - **Bottle:** batch code.
  - **Status:** `owned`, `wishlist` or `gone` (finished, sold, gave away). [my call, after builder review] "Gone" is a one-tap action on the item page (no upkeep beyond that). Gone items leave the Owned list and Today, but keep their diary history and still count in "smells like" for wishlist reasons ("loved the sample").
  - **Overrides:** per-field overrides of the shared perfume record.
  - **Photos.**
  - **Wishlist items:** these are `items` with status `wishlist` and no photos required. "Move to owned" flips the status.
- **`sets`**, private to the collection: house, set name, photo(s). Items link to a set.
- **`wears`**: collection, item, **perfume** (copied from the item when the wear is logged), date, time of day (optional), 1–5 star rating (optional), notes (optional). Several per day are allowed. [my call, after builder review] Keeping the perfume on the wear means notes survive if an item is deleted (the item link becomes null), and a perfume page shows notes from every item of that scent (e.g. a sample and later a full bottle).
- **Collection grouping:** items of the same perfume group into one expandable row, as Seeds groups packets by variety. The perfume page is per perfume, with its items listed. [my call]
- **`scans` / `scan_sessions` / photos:** as in Seeds. A scan has a mode (`bottle` / `samples` / `set`). One samples or set photo produces **several** results, each reviewed separately, grouped by the source photo.
- **Provenance:** each field is labelled *read from bottle*, *AI-suggested* or *edited*. [seeds]
- **RLS:** everything except `perfumes` belongs to a collection, and RLS allows access to members of that collection. All members can view, add, edit and delete everything. The shared table is written only by the server, with the same protections as Seeds' shared catalogue.

## 1. Capture

- **Adding without a photo** (for wishlist items, gap suggestions, or logging a wear of something never scanned): type the house and name. Claude identifies the perfume and fills in its details, and the result goes through the same review step as a scan, labelled "from text, not label". [user]
  - A perfume added from text alone is **private to the collection** until a real label photo confirms it. The shared catalogue only learns from photos, as in Seeds (migrations 00011, 00017). [my call]

- **The scan icon** opens the camera. A switch at the top picks the mode: **Bottle · Samples · Set**. [user modes; switch my call]
- **Bottle mode:**
  - Hold a bottle in the square guide. It captures automatically when the picture is sharp and steady, using Seeds' detector heuristics as the starting point, then plays a soft pop and flashes. [user, seeds]
  - **No vibration on iPhone** (Safari doesn't support it). Vibrate on Android where it's available. [seeds]
  - **One photo per bottle by default.** A "+ back / box" button adds a second photo before moving on. [my call]
  - The manual shutter is always available. Auto-capture pauses after about 20 seconds without a new bottle. [seeds]
- **Samples mode:** one photo of several labelled vials, taken with the manual shutter at full resolution. Extraction returns a list, and each vial becomes its own review result. [user; manual + full-resolution my call]
- **Set mode:**
  - Photograph the box, with an optional second photo of the insert card.
  - Extraction returns the house, the set name and the list of scents. This creates **a set + one sample item per scent, linked to the set**. [user]
  - In review the user can untick vials they don't have. [my call]
- **Not a perfume:** photos that aren't of perfume are discarded automatically. [seeds]
- **Upload:** as in Seeds, with one automatic retry and errors shown inline. Bottle photos are 1600 px JPEGs. Samples and set photos are kept at full resolution. Thumbnails are 240 px WebP. [seeds + my call]

## 2. Identification and details

- **Extraction:** reads the house, the name, the concentration and anything else printed on the label, using Seeds' per-field status (`clear` / `unclear` / `absent`). It also returns whether the photo is perfume at all. [seeds]
- **Identification:** matches an existing `perfumes` row or proposes a new one. Bottle shape counts as evidence alongside the label.
- **Enrichment:** fills empty `perfumes` fields with AI-suggested values for notes, family, accords, longevity, sillage, seasons, time of day, occasions, perfumer, year and typical price. These are labelled "AI-suggested". Names are fenced in the prompt and the output is cleaned. [user, seeds]
- **Review queue:**
  - Bulk-approve confident results. A result is flagged when identity fields are `unclear`, when it's a possible duplicate of something owned, or when nothing matches. [seeds]
  - **Samples and set photos are reviewed as a group**, showing the whole photo with no crops. [my call]

## 3. Collection

- **Switch:** **Owned · Sets · Wishlist**. [user + my call]
- **Search:** full text across house, name, notes and accords.
- **Filter sheet:** as in Seeds: family, season, time of day, occasion, house, format, set, rating, worn recently / neglected. [seeds pattern]
- **List rows:** a thumbnail, the house in small capitals, the name in Bodoni, and the pyramid motif.
- **Perfume page:**
  - **Facts:** the hero photo, the facts, the scent pyramid with notes, longevity and sillage.
  - **Your stats:** times worn and average rating.
  - **Smells like (on your shelf):** owned items only, each with a one-line reason. [my call]
  - **Layer with:** owned items only, each with a one-line reason. [my call]
  - **Your notes:** every wear with notes or a rating, newest first. [user]
  - **Actions:** a "Wear today" button, plus edit and delete (with confirmation).
- **Sets:** the set page lists its samples, marking each tried or untried (tried means it has at least one wear).

## 4. Today [user]

- **Context line:** the day, the time, the season and the live weather (temperature and conditions) from Open-Meteo, for the user's location.
- **Three picks,** each with a short reason ("Heavy base · big sillage", "Not worn in 6 weeks", "Untried sample").
- **Nudges:** "Something lighter / Something bolder", and an occasion setting (office, casual, date, evening out, gym…).
- **Wear** on a pick logs it in the diary in one tap, then offers to add a rating and note.
- **How picks are chosen** [my call, from the brainstorm]:
  - **Rules shortlist** candidates by suitability for the season, time of day and temperature, since heat increases projection.
  - Candidates are **re-ranked by rotation:** recently worn items rank lower, and untried samples and neglected items get a boost.
  - **The model writes the one-line reason** for each pick and can break ties. If the model is unavailable, the rules alone still produce picks.
- **Without a location:** use the season and time of day only, and show a one-line prompt to set a location.
- **Notices:** the review-queue notice sits above the picks, as in Seeds.

## 5. Diary [user]

- **Logging:**
  - Log a wear from Today, from a perfume page, or from the Diary itself (search your collection).
  - Pick a date (default today). The rating (1–5 stars) and notes are optional.
  - Several wears per day are allowed. [my call]
- **Month view:** a calendar grid with a small bottle mark per day. The mark is coloured by scent family, and days with more than one wear show a count. Tap a day to see that day's entries; tap an entry to open the perfume.
- **Year view:** **later.** A 12-month heatmap in the same style, with each day clickable.
- **Edit and delete** your own wear entries.

## 6. Stats [user]

- **Totals:** bottles, samples, sets.
- **Estimated value:** uses the purchase price where entered, otherwise typical retail price × size. It's clearly labelled an estimate. [my call]
- **Breakdowns:** by scent family, by season, by house, and by format.
- **Worn vs. neglected:** the number not worn in 3 months, with a link to a filtered collection. Also shows the most worn and the highest rated.
- **Gaps:** cards such as "Nothing fresh or aquatic for hot days", with buttons to see suggestions and to add to the wishlist. Suggestions come from the model and are labelled as such.

## 7. Settings

- Location (postcode or geolocation; only approximate coordinates are stored).
- Units (ml).
- Account, sign out, and delete account.

## Phasing (proposal) [my call]

- **Phase 0, throwaway spike:**
  - Camera auto-capture on real bottles (glare, curves).
  - A busy samples photo with 8–10 vials.
  - One discovery-set box.
  - Extraction accuracy on these. Keep the fixtures for evals.
- **Phase 1:** foundation (auth, schema/RLS, design system, PWA shell), then capture + extraction + review, then collection + perfume page.
- **Phase 2:** Today, the diary (month view) and stats.
- **Phase 3:** wishlist, gaps, smells like, layering, and the diary year view.
